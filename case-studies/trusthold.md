# TrustHold

Peer-to-peer escrow aimed at Gumtree and Facebook Marketplace deals.

## Problem

Classifieds buyers and sellers do not trust each other with payment or delivery. They needed a hold on the money until the handoff was accepted, on the phone as well as on the web.

## What I built

- Web client for a buyer and a seller to open and progress a hold
- Capacitor shells for iOS and Android, packaged toward App Store and Play submission
- App identity, store checklist, and marketing copy kept with the mobile package

## Stack

Web client, Capacitor, iOS and Android projects.

## Status

Private product. Store packaging is prepared. This page does not include API hosts, account names, or payment configuration.
